# 2026-05-14 — Autopsy DNA: Structural Business Failure Diagnosis

## Canonical positioning

Autopsy is not a quiz app.
Autopsy is not generic business coaching.
Autopsy is not startup theatre.

Autopsy is a structural business failure diagnosis system.

## Core doctrine

The system must diagnose how a business fails structurally before the operator wastes time, money, confidence, or reputation.

It must identify:

- the primary failure dimension
- the likely collapse pathway
- the speed of failure
- the visibility of the failure
- recoverability
- invalid evidence
- evidence required for progression
- retest conditions
- recovery validation

## Diagnostic stance

Autopsy asks:

- What will break first?
- Why will it break?
- How fast will it break?
- Can it be recovered?
- What evidence proves recovery?
- What must the operator do before progressing?

## Non-negotiable design rule

Every Autopsy output must explain structural failure mechanics, not merely produce a score.

A verdict without failure mechanics is incomplete.
A score without recovery evidence is weak.
A recommendation without retest criteria is not acceptable.

## Language rule

Use plain operational language.
Avoid startup theatre, consultant abstraction, and MBA language.

The intended user may be an inexperienced operator starting a cleaning or service business. The system must be serious without being obscure.

## Core phrase

Structural business failure diagnosis is now part of Autopsy DNA.

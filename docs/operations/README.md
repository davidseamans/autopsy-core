## Governing Rule

A document is executable operational authority only when it:

- declares a current status, owner, effective date, and supersession rule;
- aligns with DS-001 and the active stack: Supabase, GitHub, n8n, Vercel, Notion, and bounded OpenAI capability;
- identifies current dependencies, authority, recovery, observability, and retained certification evidence; and
- has been independently verified against its required business outcome.

A filename such as `v1` does not establish current authority.

A replacement recovery specification or runbook must be explicitly certified under DS-001 before operational use. It must use the current Vercel-delivered application architecture, validated Supabase authority, canonical GitHub source, governed n8n recovery paths, Notion operational controls, and retained positive, negative, idempotency, dependency, reconciliation, and restore evidence.

Until that replacement is certified, recovery documentation remains **AMBER** and no historical runbook may be represented as GREEN.

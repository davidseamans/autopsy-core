# Product Insight — Retest Condition Breakthrough

## Date

2026-05-13

## Accepted Line

Retest only after meaningful operational change, scaling pressure, or structural drift is detected.

## Why It Works

This line reframes retest from a repeat quiz into a progression control.

It gives low-scoring candidates something concrete to work toward upfront:

- change something real
- produce evidence
- face operational pressure
- then retest

It also gives high-scoring candidates a disciplined reason not to keep retesting pointlessly:

- retest when conditions change
- retest when scale introduces pressure
- retest when drift appears

## Product Meaning

Retest is not entertainment.

Retest is earned by changed evidence or changed operating conditions.

## Strategic Value

This supports Autopsy as a gatekeeper:

- permission is conditional
- progression requires proof
- results are not static motivation
- retest is tied to reality, not curiosity

## Future Worksheet Connection

The worksheet should define what counts as meaningful operational change for each verdict, score range, dimension, and operator profile.

Examples:

- score 0–6: produce basic survival proof
- score 10–17: repair dominant constraint and collect evidence
- score 18–24: prove consistency under controlled operation
- score 25–30: monitor drift, scale pressure, whale risk, and governance integrity

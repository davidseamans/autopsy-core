# Founder Psychology Notes — Autopsy Landing Page

## Date

2026-05-13

## User Experience Insight

A common founder segment is young couples, often recently married, with modest jobs, looking to get into their own business.

They may be motivated by freedom, frustration, entitlement narratives, or optimism rather than evidence.

In Australia, there is a perceived cultural/government-supported mindset that everyone is entitled to get into business. Autopsy should challenge that premise without becoming hostile.

## Partner / Couple Dynamic

Autopsy can help clarify arguments between partners before money and obligation are committed.

Common pattern:

- one partner is enthusiastic
- the other partner is pulled into admin/bookkeeping
- later conflict emerges: “this was not my idea”
- bookkeeper/spouse carries consequences of weak planning

## Landing Page Angle

Use controlled fear, not theatrical fear.

Possible positioning:

- Before you rope your partner into the bookkeeping, test the business.
- Autopsy gives both partners the same evidence before the argument starts.
- If one person wants the business and the other has to carry the admin, the evidence needs to be visible before commitment.
- A business decision made by optimism becomes a household problem when cash, work, and stress arrive.

## Guardrail

Do not mock founders.

Do not sound anti-aspiration.

The tone should be protective, evidence-based, and reality-facing.

Autopsy should challenge entitlement without humiliating the user.

## Strong Landing Page Use

This insight belongs in a section such as:

`Before the business becomes a household problem`

or

`For couples making the decision together`

## Strategic Value

This angle may be commercially powerful because it speaks to hidden buyer pain:

- family pressure
- partner disagreement
- unpaid admin labour
- emotional risk
- household financial exposure

It broadens Autopsy beyond business logic into decision protection.

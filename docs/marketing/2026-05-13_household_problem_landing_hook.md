# Landing Hook — Household Problem

## Date

2026-05-13

## Accepted Line

Before the business becomes a household problem, test the evidence together.

## Why It Works

This line speaks to a real founder segment:

- young couples
- recently married partners
- modest jobs
- first-time business optimism
- one enthusiastic partner
- one partner pulled into admin/bookkeeping
- household stress once cash, workload, and reality arrive

## Use Case

This belongs in the founder/operator landing page as a secondary emotional section, not the hero.

Suggested section title:

`Before the business becomes a household problem`

Suggested body:

`A business decision is rarely just a business decision. If one person wants the business and the other has to carry the admin, bookkeeping, cash pressure, or cleanup, the evidence should be visible before the commitment.`

`Autopsy gives both partners the same operating evidence before money, time, and household stress are committed.`

## Guardrail

Use controlled fear, not theatrical fear.

Do not mock founders.
Do not attack aspiration.
Do not sound anti-business.

Tone should be protective, evidence-based, and reality-facing.

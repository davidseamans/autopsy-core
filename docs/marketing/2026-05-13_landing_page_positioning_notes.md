# Landing Page Positioning Notes — Autopsy

## Date

2026-05-13

## Core Position

Autopsy is no longer positioned as a simple viability quiz.

It is a gatekeeper that tests whether a business idea, acquisition, or early-stage operation has earned the right to proceed.

## Key Strategic Questions

### Multiple Landing Pages / ABCD Testing

Rotating multiple landing pages is strategically attractive but should not be the first implementation.

First create one canonical positioning page, then test variants later.

Suggested variants for future testing:

- Founder fear / do not start unproven
- Consultant/client diagnostic
- Acquisition/investor evidence test
- Guarantee/progression/excellence positioning

### Analytics / Scroll Depth

Future analytics should measure:

- visits
- conversion to Run Autopsy
- scroll depth
- CTA clicks
- drop-off by section
- completion from landing page to verdict

Do not overbuild analytics before traffic exists.

### Core/Sleeve Timing Risk

Autopsy may be ready before Core and Sleeve.

This is acceptable if messaging is honest:

- Autopsy is available now as the diagnostic/gatekeeper.
- Core/Sleeve is the progression architecture under development.
- Do not promise immediate Core/Sleeve delivery before it exists.

Position near-term product as:

`Autopsy identifies readiness, constraints, and proof obligations. Future progression pathways will connect evidence-ready operators into Core/Sleeve systems.`

## Guardrail

Do not sell what is not built.

Use Autopsy's strength now:

- evidence
- constraint discovery
- verdict authority
- worksheet proof obligations
- retest logic

Core/Sleeve becomes a roadmap and progression destination, not a false immediate promise.

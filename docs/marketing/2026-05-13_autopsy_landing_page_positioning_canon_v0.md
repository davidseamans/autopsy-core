# Autopsy Landing Page Positioning Canon v0

## Date

2026-05-13

## Purpose

Create the canonical positioning spine for the Autopsy landing page before building, rotating, or testing variants.

## Core Position

Autopsy is not a viability quiz.

Autopsy is a gatekeeper that tests whether a business idea, acquisition, or early-stage operation has earned the right to proceed.

## Primary Message

Do not start a business you have not proven.

## Secondary Message

Autopsy tests the evidence before you commit money, time, reputation, or complexity.

It identifies the primary constraint, explains how the business will break under pressure, and gives the proof required before progression is trusted.

## Brand Dictums

- A system that does not let people lie to themselves.
- False confidence is expensive.
- Autopsy is a gatekeeper, not a cheerleader.
- If the evidence is weak, encouragement is dangerous.
- A good idea still fails if the operator cannot execute.
- Progression is earned by proof, not intention.
- Do not scale what you have not proven.
- The verdict is not opinion. It is permission control.
- The goal is not to pass a quiz. The goal is to prove the business can survive reality.

## Anti-Promise

Autopsy does not promise that every idea can be rescued.

If the evidence is weak, the result must say so.

## Who It Is For

- People starting a business who want evidence before commitment.
- People buying a business who want to test the operating reality before acquisition.
- Consultants/coaches/advisers who need a structured way to expose the real constraint before recommending action.
- Serious operators willing to face proof, not comfort.

## Who It Is Not For

- People looking for encouragement.
- People who want a prettier business plan.
- People who confuse intention with evidence.
- People unwilling to be stopped by weak proof.

## What It Tests

### Cash Reality
Can the business survive on real cash, not hope, invoices, or guesses?

### Economic Literacy
Does the operator understand margin, costs, pricing, and what must be true for the job to make money?

### Market Reality
Is there real demand from real buyers, proven by behaviour rather than opinion?

### Operational Capacity
Can the work be delivered consistently without chaos, missed standards, or overloading the operator?

### Execution Discipline
Does the operator reliably do the required work, follow through, record evidence, and correct problems?

### Psychological Resilience
Can the operator handle pressure, rejection, fatigue, uncertainty, and correction without avoiding reality?

## What The User Receives

- score and verdict
- run details
- dimension pressure profile
- primary constraint
- mechanical failure chain
- what this verdict means
- execution diagnosis
- mechanism steps
- final outcome
- worksheet
- retest condition

## Verdict Positioning

### Not Viable
Stop. Current evidence does not support progression.

### High Risk
Proceed only through controlled repair and retesting.

### Viable
Proceed cautiously with proof collection and operating discipline.

### Structurally Viable
Ready for controlled progression, provided governance and telemetry remain intact.

## Worksheet Positioning

Autopsy does not end with a verdict.

The worksheet defines what proof is required before the next step is trusted.

Low scores demand survival proof.

High scores demand excellence, governance, telemetry, drift control, Pareto focus, and whale-risk awareness.

## Core/Sleeve Roadmap Position

Do not sell what is not built.

Autopsy is available as the diagnostic/gatekeeper.

Core/Sleeve should be positioned as future progression architecture, not an immediate promise.

Safe wording:

Autopsy identifies readiness, constraints, and proof obligations. Future progression pathways will connect evidence-ready operators into Core/Sleeve systems.

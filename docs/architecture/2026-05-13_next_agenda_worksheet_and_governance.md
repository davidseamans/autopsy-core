## Worksheet Direction

Move from generic worksheet to verdict-sensitive and operator-sensitive proof architecture.

### Not Viable / Very Low Score

Ask for basic survival proof:

- how many clients can be acquired?
- what can be charged?
- what proof exists that buyers will pay?
- what cash runway exists?
- what minimum execution rhythm can be proven?

### High Risk

Require dominant-constraint repair:

- evidence collection
- correction cycles
- controlled retest
- no scaling until proof is produced

### Viable

Require controlled operating proof:

- early telemetry
- repeatability
- margin validation
- operational consistency
- Pareto focus

### Structurally Viable / Experienced Operator

Move toward excellence and guarantee readiness:

- how to outperform industry averages
- how to push average value upward
- concentration risk / whale exposure
- operational telemetry
- governance discipline
- Core/Sleeve handoff readiness

## Strategic Concepts To Introduce

- E-Myth: repeatable system, not heroic operator effort
- Pareto: focus on the vital few causes, clients, constraints, jobs, and behaviours
- Whale: avoid dangerous dependence on one major client, lead source, person, job type, or cost assumption
- Guarantee readiness: evidence strong enough to justify progression

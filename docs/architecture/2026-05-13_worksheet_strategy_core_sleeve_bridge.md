# Worksheet Strategy — Core/Sleeve Bridge

## Date

2026-05-13

## Strategic Position

Worksheet must evolve from generic advice into the bridge between Autopsy and Core/Sleeve.

Autopsy diagnoses readiness and permission.
Worksheet defines proof obligations and progression gates.
Core/Sleeve receives only operators who have produced evidence.

## Core Principle

Autopsy is not a questionnaire. It is a gatekeeper.

Worksheet is not homework. It is proof architecture.

## Direction

Worksheet output should become:

- score-sensitive
- verdict-sensitive
- dimension-sensitive
- operator-profile-sensitive
- industry-sleeve-aware

But not all at once.

## Commercial Doctrine

The product is not ordinary accounting advice.

An experienced investor scoring 30/30 does not need generic proof requests that their accountant would already ask for.

The package must point toward:

- excellence
- guarantee readiness
- operational proof
- Core/Sleeve progression
- E-Myth operating discipline
- Pareto focus
- Whale/client concentration awareness

## Future Worksheet Layers

### 1. E-Myth Layer

Does the operator have a repeatable system, or are they still personally carrying the business?

### 2. Pareto Layer

Which few causes, clients, jobs, constraints, or behaviours will drive most of the result?

### 3. Whale Layer

Is the business exposed to one oversized customer, lead source, employee, job type, or cost dependency?

### 4. Core/Sleeve Bridge

If proof is produced, what Core/Sleeve module should the operator move into next?

Examples:

- lead/proof weakness → Leads & Pipeline
- pricing/margin weakness → Quotes & Jobs / Pricing Sleeve
- delivery weakness → Scheduling / QA Sleeve
- execution weakness → Time & Attendance / Operating Rhythm
- cash weakness → Payroll Inputs / Expenses / Cash Reality controls

## Near-Term Recommendation

Do not immediately redesign backend worksheet generation.

Next step should be a design directive defining worksheet maturity levels:

- Not Viable: stop + proof before movement
- High Risk: repair dominant constraint + retest
- Viable: controlled execution + evidence collection
- Structurally Viable: progression readiness + Core/Sleeve handoff

## Guardrail

Do not let worksheet become generic consulting advice.

Every worksheet item should answer:

- what proof is required?
- what evidence is invalid?
- what action creates proof?
- what gate allows retest or progression?
- what Core/Sleeve capability does this connect to?

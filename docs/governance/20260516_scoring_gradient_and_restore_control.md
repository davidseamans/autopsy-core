## Gradient doctrine
A score of 14 is not 15. A score of 15 is not 16. Close scores must change tone, urgency, permission, and retest burden.

Required gradient examples:
- 14: unstable, constrained, likely blocked unless evidence improves
- 15: threshold condition, conditional progression only with proof
- 16: still high-risk, but less collapse-imminent if dominant constraint is controlled

The restored system must demonstrate:
- hard_fail override works
- 10-question flow works
- score gradient changes language
- 14/15/16 produce different outputs
- 24/25 produce different outputs
- 30/30 does not invent a primary constraint

## Governance consequence
No future restore is accepted until the above proof exists.

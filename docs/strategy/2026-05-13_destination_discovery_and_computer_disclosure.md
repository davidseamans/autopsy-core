# Destination Discovery + Computer Disclosure

## Date

2026-05-13

## Core Insight

Many operators will disclose more honestly to a structured computer screen than to a human adviser.

People may avoid telling an expert their age, weight, weakness, fear, ignorance, or motivation, yet enter the same information into a neutral form without resistance.

## User Experience Insight

The user has substantial business experience, including accounting, running multiple businesses, scientific/empirical interest in business behaviour, and two bankruptcies.

Despite that credibility, people often resist direct human challenge.

Observed pattern:

- women often appreciated expert reality-checking
- men often presented as cowboys with forward momentum and ego
- direct advice could cause silence or shutdown
- sensitive destination/motivation questions may be better handled by structured digital intake than human confrontation

## Product Implication

Autopsy/Core/Sleeve should use structured computer-led disclosure to extract destination data without making the user feel personally judged.

The system can ask questions a human adviser cannot ask as efficiently or safely.

## Destination Discovery Requirement

Future pathway intake should identify what the operator is actually trying to create:

- survival income
- lifestyle autonomy
- family security
- passive ownership
- growth and scale
- acquisition improvement
- exit value
- challenge/excellence
- industry leadership
- legacy

## Design Principle

Do not make this feel like therapy or dream coaching.

Make it operational:

`What outcome must this business produce for the effort and risk to be worth it?`

## Core/Sleeve Translation

Declared destination should route the operator into different system pathways:

- Survival pathway
- Lifestyle pathway
- Scale pathway
- Passive ownership pathway
- Acquisition pathway
- Exit-readiness pathway
- Excellence pathway

## Guardrail

Do not assume money is the universal motive.

Do not guarantee arbitrary financial outcomes.

Do not let the system prescribe progression before it understands the operator's desired destination.
